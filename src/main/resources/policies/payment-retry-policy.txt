PAYMENT RETRY POLICY

Policy ID: RETRY-001

Purpose:
Define when a failed payment may be retried.

Rules:

1. Payments with INSUFFICIENT_FUNDS may be retried.

2. The first retry should occur after a minimum delay of 24 hours.

3. A payment must not be retried more than 3 times.

4. A retry must not be performed immediately after the original failure.

5. If the maximum retry count has been reached, the system must not
   automatically retry the payment.

6. Failed retries should result in customer notification.

7. Payments above INR 20,000 require additional review before
   automated recovery actions.

Allowed recovery actions:
- RETRY_PAYMENT
- CUSTOMER_NOTIFICATION

Prohibited actions:
- Unlimited retries
- Immediate repeated retries
- Automatic cancellation of high-value subscriptions
