HIGH VALUE PAYMENT POLICY

Policy ID: HIGH-VALUE-001

Purpose:
Protect high-value transactions from unsafe automated recovery.

Rules:

1. Payments greater than INR 20,000 are considered high-value.

2. High-value payments must not receive unrestricted automated retries.

3. High-value recovery cases require additional decision validation.

4. The AI recovery agent must not authorize an action that violates
   this policy.

5. When policy confidence is insufficient, the case must be escalated
   for human review.

Allowed actions:
- HUMAN_REVIEW
- CUSTOMER_NOTIFICATION

Restricted actions:
- RETRY_PAYMENT
