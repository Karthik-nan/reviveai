CUSTOMER NOTIFICATION POLICY

Policy ID: NOTIFY-001

Purpose:
Define when customers should be notified about payment failures.

Rules:

1. Customers should be notified when a payment failure creates a
   recovery case.

2. The notification should explain that the payment was unsuccessful.

3. The notification must not expose internal error information.

4. Customers should be informed when an automatic retry is scheduled.

5. If automatic recovery is exhausted, the customer should receive
   a final notification.

Allowed actions:
- CUSTOMER_NOTIFICATION
